TIDEBOUND

Sail a little cargo boat from island to island. Read the signs, ride the ramps, tag the dolphins and keep off the rocks.

APP STORE · SOON GOOGLE PLAY · SOON LEADERBOARDS

THE AIM

Deliver the crate to each island's pier. Collect coins, pearls and relics on the way, keep your hull in one piece, and beat your best time. Three rock hits and the boat sinks.

HOW TO PLAY

  1. 1Start the engineTap Start Engine, then set your speed with the throttle.
  2. 2SteerTurn the wheel, or slide a finger anywhere on the water.
  3. 3Read the signEach island has six channels. Take the one the sign shows and follow the coin trail to stay clear of the rocks.
  4. 4DockLine up with the pier to deliver the crate and bank your coins.
